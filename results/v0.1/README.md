# PsychSafe-Eval v0.1 results

This directory contains the official v0.1 administration and evaluation outputs.

- `administration/` contains public normalized records of the four official administrations and their administration metadata. The exact submitted-message trajectories and substantive outputs are retained; provider-native envelopes and operational metadata are omitted. See `administration/RAW_EXECUTION_PROVENANCE.md`.
- `scoring/UNBLINDED_SCORING_RECORD.json` is the finalized post-evaluation scoring record after blinded scoring was complete and identities were revealed.
- `scoring/UNBLINDING_RECORD_2026-09-22.md` documents unblinding.
- `aggregates/` contains validated derived outputs for dimension profiles, Critical Safety Events, case-level findings, and Enrichment Events.

PsychSafe-Eval does not report an overall composite score. Critical Safety Events are reported as raw counts, and Enrichment Events are descriptive/non-scored.
